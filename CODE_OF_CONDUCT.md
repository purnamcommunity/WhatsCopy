# Code Of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want WhatsCopy to be a respectful, practical, and welcoming open-source project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but be clear about technical concerns.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Accept feedback gracefully.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Repeated disruptive conduct.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.
